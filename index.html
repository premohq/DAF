Up to 8 deals. Type prices like 14.99, or 2 for 41.99. The tag is optional: NEW shows in ember red, anything else in gold. A store photo behind the list is blurred and darkened so the prices stay readable.

The last slide of a carousel or a Story: the Dante’s Liquors logo and where to find the store, with a 21+ line at the bottom.

6 · Export

The ZIP holds the 4:5, 1:1, 9:16 and 16:9 PNGs plus a caption.txt. Video renders frame by frame into a smooth MP4 (older browsers record in real time instead, as MP4 or WebM). GIF exports have no audio and produce larger files; video is recommended for Reels. Audio can be added in the Instagram app before posting.

7 · Caption & hashtags

Written from the ad’s own text and price, so you can edit it before copying. Every caption ends with “21+ only. Please drink responsibly.” If you boost a post as a paid ad, set the audience to 21+.
