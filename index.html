Get the free Google key (2 minutes)
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com/expressmode and sign in with a personal Gmail account that has never used Google Cloud.
  2. Accept the terms. Google sets up Vertex AI express mode and shows you an API key. Copy it.
  3. Paste it above with Vertex AI · free 90 days selected.

Free for 90 days within Google's limits, no card needed, and Google's terms allow commercial use. One free period per person, so don't open extra accounts to restart it. After that, turning on billing costs roughly 7–15¢ per image. The key stays in this browser and is only ever sent to Google.
