4 · Engine

No setup. You sign in to a free Puter account once, and every account gets a free monthly allowance. Puter's terms cover personal and demo use, so switch to a Google key for paid client ads.

Get the free Google key (2 minutes)
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com/expressmode and sign in with a personal Gmail account that has never used Google Cloud.
  2. Accept the terms. Google sets up Vertex AI express mode and shows you an API key. Copy it.
  3. Paste it above with Vertex AI · free 90 days selected.

Free for 90 days within Google's limits, no card needed, and Google's terms allow commercial use. One free period per person, so don't open extra accounts to restart it. After that, turning on billing costs roughly 7–15¢ per image. The key stays in this browser and is only ever sent to Google.

5 · Price

Dark card with gold keyline, per the approved brand style. The caption sits above the card in small gold capitals. Turn the card off for image-only ads.

5 · Deals

Up to 8 deals. Type prices like 14.99, or 2 for 41.99. The tag is optional: NEW shows in ember red, anything else in gold. A store photo behind the list is blurred and darkened so the prices stay readable.

4 · Closing slide

The last slide of a carousel or a Story: the Dante’s Liquors logo and where to find the store, with a 21+ line at the bottom.

6 · Export

The ZIP holds the 4:5, 1:1, 9:16 and 16:9 PNGs plus a caption.txt. Video renders frame by frame into a smooth MP4 (older browsers record in real time instead, as MP4 or WebM). GIF exports have no audio and produce larger files; video is recommended for Reels. Audio can be added in the Instagram app before posting.

7 · Caption & hashtags

Written from the ad’s own text and price, so you can edit it before copying. Every caption ends with “21+ only. Please drink responsibly.” If you boost a post as a paid ad, set the audience to 21+.
